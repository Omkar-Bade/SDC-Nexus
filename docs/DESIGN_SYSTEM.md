# SDC Nexus — Design System & UI Specification

## Design Aesthetic
SDC Nexus adopts a **dark, minimal, developer-oriented, editorial aesthetic** inspired by Linear, Apple, GitHub, Vercel, and Raycast.

---

## Central Color Palette
- **Background Dark**: `#0A0A0B`
- **Surface Dark**: `#14151A`
- **Card Background**: `#121318`
- **SDC Orange (Primary Accent)**: `#FF5500`
- **Restrained Blue Accent**: `#0070F3`
- **Text Primary**: `#F3F4F6`
- **Text Muted**: `#9CA3AF` / `#6B7280`

> **Forbidden Colors**: Purple, Violet, Magenta, Pink, Lavender.

---

## Typography Hierarchy
- **Headings / Hero**: `Sora`
- **Body Text**: `Inter`
- **Numbers / Metrics / IDs**: `Space Grotesk`

---

## Glassmorphism Guidelines
- Low-opacity backgrounds: `rgba(20, 21, 26, 0.7)`
- Backdrop Blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`
- Subtle borders: `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- Main backgrounds remain dark and matte.
